The rows of `database-query` are arrays: each entry of {items} has its row in `data`. An array knows nothing of its table, so the title says where its tag is edited (edit="table:uid:field"), and headliner:text names the record and the field in the headings of the rich text (the table is found from the inline field of the content element, the field is the one rich text field of the table). With `record-transformation` the rows would be records and neither would be needed.
