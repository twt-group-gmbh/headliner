The rows of `database-query` are arrays: each entry of {items} has its row in `data`. headliner:headline.row makes a record of the row (the table is found from the inline field of the content element), so the title is the one of a record: the text from the label field, the tag from the tag field, the mark for the page module from the table and the uid. headliner:text names the record and the field in the headings of the rich text. With `record-transformation` the rows would be records and headliner:headline would do.
